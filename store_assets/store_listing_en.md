## Full description (4000 karakter sınırı)

Teaching yourself to draw? Artora keeps you from spending years going in the
wrong direction.

Upload your 3 most recent drawings and Artora's AI determines your starting
level across 7 core skills: anatomy, perspective, light & shadow, proportion,
line quality, composition, and color. From there, a game-like skill tree guides
you through a curated curriculum of free video lessons — in the right order.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Upload your last 3 drawings — the AI maps your strengths and weak spots.
2. Follow the skill tree — each node has hand-picked video lessons from
   respected art educators.
3. Upload your practice work — get an instant, free "redline" analysis:
   concrete markers placed right on your drawing, showing what works and
   what to try next.
4. Earn XP, level up, and watch your ability chart grow.

WHY ARTORA IS DIFFERENT

• Feedback is always constructive — never harsh, never discouraging.
• No style bias: manga, cartoon, or realism — you're coached within YOUR
  style, not pushed toward someone else's.
• Every observation comes with an actionable suggestion, not vague criticism.
• Your progress journey keeps every assignment with its AI notes, so you can
  literally see how far you've come.

FREE DURING BETA

All AI analyses are free (with a fair daily limit). A marketplace of human
mentors is coming in a future release.

Start from where you are. Improve with every drawing.
